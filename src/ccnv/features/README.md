# `ccnv.features`

## Overview

The `ccnv.features` package implements in-memory feature extraction, genomic binning and rebinning, cohort validation and 1 Mb super-block DESeq2 depth normalization, covariate masking and feature standardization, cohort and single-sample sex classification, cell-free DNA fragmentomic transformations, and non-invasive prenatal (NIPT) reference and inference workflows (`FFY`, `FFA`, `FFX`, `FF_size`, 4D erfc ray-marginalized SCA, reference-side 24-band Fisher read weighting, Student-$`t_4`$ IRLS active FF fusion, Fisher-weighted `effective_ff`, male-fetus 5 kb `chrY` MSY normalization, and 5-state self-referenced origin engine).

## Interface

- [`binning.py:bin_intervals`](./binning.py), [`build_coordinate_grid`](./binning.py), [`compute_bin_exposure`](./binning.py), [`align_grid_indices`](./binning.py), [`align_sample_to_grid_indices`](./binning.py), [`rebin_counts`](./binning.py), & [`rebin_counts_matrix`](./binning.py): Generate 0-based half-open interval partitions, fractional bin exposures, $`O(N)`$ coordinate grid index mappings, and mass-conserving 1D/2D cumulative knot rebinning.
- [`rebinning.py:rebin_sample_buffers`](./rebinning.py): Rebins `SampleBuffers` in memory, using a vectorized NumPy `np.add.reduceat` fast path across all 7 channels on contained integer multiples and count-only cumulative knot interpolation on non-nested grids.
- [`cohort.py:Cohort`](./cohort.py), [`alloc_cohort_counts`](./cohort.py), [`load_and_assemble_cohort`](./cohort.py), [`validate_cohort`](./cohort.py), & [`rebin_cohort`](./cohort.py): Stream and validate synchronized Fortran-contiguous `float32` cohort matrices (spilling to `np.memmap` above `max_batch_memory_mb`).
- [`cohort.py:cohort_from_fasta`](./cohort.py): Builds a 0-sample coordinate grid from a contig-length `Mapping[str, int]` for flat FASTA reference models.
- [`cohort.py:build_fisher_weighted_cohort`](./cohort.py): Applies reference-side 24-band Fisher read weights ($`w^{\ast}_{0.08}`$) and pooled per-bin Kish shot-noise inflation factors across cohort sample `frag_bands` for prenatal CNV reference branches.
- [`cohort.py:estimate_size_factors`](./cohort.py) & [`mask_low_coverage_per_contig`](./cohort.py): Compute DESeq2 median-of-ratios library size factors over 1 Mb super-blocks and flag usable bins per contig exceeding 5% of positive median contig coverage.
- [`sex.py:SexInference`](./sex.py), [`SexLabel`](./sex.py), [`infer_germline_sex_2d`](./sex.py), [`infer_sample_sexes`](./sex.py), [`infer_single_sample_sex`](./sex.py), & [`par_xtr_mask`](./sex.py): Classify germline `XX` vs. `XY` cohort and single-sample karyotypes via PAR/XTR-masked non-PAR dosage separation.
- [`covariates.py:validate_min_mappability`](./covariates.py), [`mask_covariates`](./covariates.py), & [`covariate_features`](./covariates.py): Validate mappability bounds, mask low-mappability/high-N/invalid-GC bins, and assemble standardized exogenous covariate matrices for Prototype Graph clustering.
- [`normalization.py:BranchModel`](./normalization.py), [`BranchNormalizationResult`](./normalization.py), [`shrink_kish_kappa`](./normalization.py), [`estimate_adjacent_dispersion_tau`](./normalization.py), [`align_reference_unmasked_bins`](./normalization.py), [`build_branch_model_from_tables`](./normalization.py), [`normalize_sample_branch`](./normalization.py), & [`normalize_branch`](./normalization.py): Align reference unmasked intervals onto sample grids, apply per-sample `PoissonRegressor` covariate bias correction, project sample coverage onto reference PCA loadings via `HuberRegressor`, evaluate within-sample Prototype Graph baselines, shrink Kish shot-noise inflation factors, estimate MAD adjacent-bin overdispersion scales, and synthesize depth-decomposed Empirical Bayes precision weights.
- [`fragmentomics.py:CohortFragmentomicsFit`](./fragmentomics.py), [`extract_sample_fragmentomics`](./fragmentomics.py), [`fit_cohort_fragmentomics_model`](./fragmentomics.py), [`apply_prenatal_fisher_weighting`](./fragmentomics.py), [`resolve_fisher_moment_weights`](./fragmentomics.py), [`has_paired_fragmentomics`](./fragmentomics.py), & [`resolve_fragmentomics_compatibility`](./fragmentomics.py): Decode `FragmentomicsMetadata`, deconvolve cohort fetal/maternal length densities on non-quarantined paired-end samples, fit the 36-feature logit-link `FF_size` ensemble, evaluate sample 24-band Fisher-weighted counts, Kish inflation factors, and Fisher-weighted moment multipliers, and enforce sample/reference pairing compatibility.
- [`prenatal_grid.py:col_f64`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`col_i64`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`sample_grid`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`resolve_contig_masks`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`prepare_covariate_multipliers`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`rebin_track_1d`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`compute_100k_profiles`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`extract_ref_contig_roles`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`resolve_5k_covariates`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`fit_sample_gc_exponent`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`compute_5k_msy_exposure`](./prenatal_grid.py), [`resolve_ffy_bin_size`](./prenatal_grid.py), & [`resolve_chry_anchor_ff`](./prenatal_grid.py): Extract typed Arrow array columns, reconstruct sample coordinate grids, align sample and reference grids, normalize GC/mappability multipliers, fit sample-adaptive 5 kb GC exponents, project CLI override tracks across bin sizes, evaluate coarse 100 kb log2 profiles, compute 5 kb diploid-equivalent MSY exposures, and resolve LOCO-guarded `chrY` fetal fraction anchors.
- [`prenatal.py:fit_prenatal_models`](./prenatal.py), [`predict_prenatal`](./prenatal.py), & [`normalize_prenatal_chry`](./prenatal.py): Fit 5 kb `chrY` closed-form Poisson MSY `FFY` (with empirical per-bin male efficiency in `null_mean` and female-anchored `yfrac_threshold`), coarsened 100 kb unmasked `FFA` PCA + logit ensemble, forward $`R_X`$, and fragmentomics models into `prenatal_autosomal` and `prenatal_ffy` branches; evaluate fetal sex, `FFY`/`FFA`/`FFX`/`FF_size`, 4D erfc ray-marginalized SCA calls, Student-$`t_4`$ IRLS fused `active_ff`, and Fisher-weighted `effective_ff`; and normalize male-fetus (`fetal_sex == "M"`) 5 kb clean MSY `chrY` bins from `prenatal_ffy` against the FF-calibrated 1-copy Poisson baseline $`\hat{\lambda}_b = e_b(s_{\text{bg}}\lambda_{\text{bg}, b} + \eta_b\frac{f}{2\kappa_{\text{cal}}})`$ with Jensen-debiased $`\log_2`$ ratios, Poisson precision weights, and propagated FF baseline variance (returning `None` for female fetuses).
- [`prenatal_origin.py:BandPrefixSums`](./prenatal_origin.py), [`PrenatalLoDMetrics`](./prenatal_origin.py), [`classify_segment_origin_5state`](./prenatal_origin.py), [`annotate_prenatal_origin_and_lod`](./prenatal_origin.py), & [`annotate_sample_prenatal_segments`](./prenatal_origin.py): Query masked-bin-excluded $`O(\log U)`$ 24-band prefix sums, evaluate stoichiometric discordance (`SCA_DISCORDANT`, `SUB_FETAL_RATIO`, `SUPER_FETAL_RATIO`), and classify biological origin via the 5-state self-referenced coupled depth + Dirichlet-Multinomial engine (`FETAL_CONSTITUTIONAL`, `PLACENTAL_MOSAIC`, `MATERNAL_UNINHERITED`, `MATERNAL_AND_FETAL_INHERITED`, `MATERNAL_MOSAIC`, with `INDETERMINATE` when max posterior $`\lt 0.80`$).

## Invariants

- [`binning.py:rebin_counts`](./binning.py) & [`rebin_counts_matrix`](./binning.py): Source and target partitions must share identical positive contig lengths and strictly increasing non-overlapping intervals arranged in contiguous per-contig runs; output counts are non-negative and conserve total read mass.
- [`rebinning.py:rebin_sample_buffers`](./rebinning.py): Rejects upsampling to a finer target bin size (`BinCompatibilityError`); non-nested coarsening redistributes `count` only while zeroing fragmentomic channels and clearing `ccnv.fragmentomics` metadata.
- [`cohort.py:validate_cohort`](./cohort.py): Rejects mixed paired-end and single-end cohorts (`ReferenceError`) as well as mismatched contig layouts or bin coordinates.
- [`sex.py:infer_germline_sex_2d`](./sex.py) & [`infer_single_sample_sex`](./sex.py): Exclude PAR1/PAR2/XTR pseudoautosomal intervals before evaluating non-PAR `chrX`/`chrY` dosage and quarantine $`4.5\sigma`$ MAD ellipse sex-chromosome aneuploid outliers from reference branch training.
- [`covariates.py:validate_min_mappability`](./covariates.py): Minimum mappability threshold must be a finite float in $`[0.0, 1.0]`$.
- [`normalization.py:normalize_sample_branch`](./normalization.py): Requires positive unmasked reference bins aligned onto the sample grid (`PredictError`) and shrinks per-bin Kish shot-noise inflation factors toward the branch median at low effective read counts.
- [`prenatal.py:fit_prenatal_models`](./prenatal.py): Requires at least one sample table, masking quarantined sex-mixture outliers and gracefully handling single-sex or unlabeled-FF cohorts.
- [`prenatal.py:normalize_prenatal_chry`](./prenatal.py): Returns `None` when `fetal_sex != "M"` or `prenatal_ffy` has no aligned usable clean MSY bins; clamps adjacent-bin MAD overdispersion guard $`\hat{\tau}_Y`$ to $`[1.0, 2.5]`$ so shot-noise variance is never underestimated.

## Side Effects

- [`cohort.py:load_and_assemble_cohort`](./cohort.py) & [`build_fisher_weighted_cohort`](./cohort.py): Read sample Parquet files across worker threads and spill large cohort matrices to temporary `np.memmap` files when exceeding `max_batch_memory_mb`.
